Question 1 — Common denominators & inequalities (6 marks)

(a) Write each pair of fractions with the same denominator. (b) Then write a suitable inequality sign (<, ≤, > or ≥) between each pair.

1a i
2/3 = 6/9 and 5/9
LCM(3, 9) = 9. Multiply top and bottom of 2/3 by 3.
1a ii
3/4 = 15/20 and 4/5 = 16/20
LCM(4, 5) = 20. 3/4 × 5/5 = 15/20; 4/5 × 4/4 = 16/20.
1a iii
5/8 = 15/24 and 7/12 = 14/24
LCM(8, 12) = 24. 5/8 × 3/3 = 15/24; 7/12 × 2/2 = 14/24.
1b i
2/3 > 5/9
Compare 6/9 and 5/9 — same denominator, so the larger numerator wins.
1b ii
3/4 < 4/5
15/20 < 16/20.
1b iii
5/8 > 7/12
15/24 > 14/24.

Question 2 — Cancel, then multiply (6 marks)

One mark per part is awarded for cancelling before multiplying, so the workings must show the cancelling step.

2a
6/9 × 3/4 × 12/36 = 1/6
Simplify each: 6/9 = 2/3, 12/36 = 1/3.
= 2/3 × 3/4 × 1/3. Cancel the 3 on top with one 3 on the bottom:
= 2 × 1 × 1 / (1 × 4 × 3) = 2/12 = 1/6.
2b
4/10 × 7/12 × 20/14 = 1/3
Simplify each: 4/10 = 2/5, 20/14 = 10/7.
= 2/5 × 7/12 × 10/7. The 7s cancel; 10/5 = 2:
= 2 × 1 × 2 / (1 × 12 × 1) = 4/12 = 1/3.
2c
11/16 × 12/24 × 18/22 = 9/32
Simplify each: 12/24 = 1/2, 18/22 = 9/11.
= 11/16 × 1/2 × 9/11. The 11s cancel:
= 1 × 1 × 9 / (16 × 2 × 1) = 9/32.

Question 3 — BIDMAS with fractions (8 marks)

Do × and ÷ before + and −. Cancel where possible.

3a
200/300 ÷ 40/90 + 3/4 = 9/4 (or 2¼)
Division first. Simplify: 200/300 = 2/3, 40/90 = 4/9.
2/3 ÷ 4/9 = 2/3 × 9/4. Cancel 3 with 9 → 3, and 2 with 4 → 2:
= 1 × 3 / (1 × 2) = 3/2.
Then 3/2 + 3/4 = 6/4 + 3/4 = 9/4.
3b
2/9 + 5/15 ÷ 6/30 = 17/9 (or 1 8/9)
Division first. Simplify: 5/15 = 1/3, 6/30 = 1/5.
1/3 ÷ 1/5 = 1/3 × 5/1 = 5/3.
Then 2/9 + 5/3 = 2/9 + 15/9 = 17/9.
3c
4/6 × 4/5 − 7/15 = 1/15
Multiplication first. 4/6 = 2/3.
2/3 × 4/5 = 8/15.
Then 8/15 − 7/15 = 1/15.
3d
10/12 − 6/8 × 10/11 = 5/33
Multiplication first. 6/8 = 3/4.
3/4 × 10/11 = 30/44 = 15/22.
Simplify 10/12 = 5/6. Common denominator of 6 and 22 is 66:
5/6 = 55/66; 15/22 = 45/66.
55/66 − 45/66 = 10/66 = 5/33.

Question 4 — Rowing club medals (2 marks)

Gold = 1/3 of medals, silver = 3/8. The rest are bronze.

4
Bronze = 7/24
Gold + silver: LCM(3, 8) = 24.
1/3 = 8/24; 3/8 = 9/24. Sum = 17/24.
Bronze = 1 − 17/24 = 24/24 − 17/24 = 7/24.

Question 5 — Anna's conversions (3 marks)

Last week: 24 out of 40. This week: 19 out of 30. Has her scoring rate improved?

5
Yes — her rate improved from 60% to ≈ 63.3%.
Convert each score to a percentage (or a decimal) for a fair comparison:
Last week: 24 ÷ 40 = 0.6 = 60%.
This week: 19 ÷ 30 = 0.6333… ≈ 63.3%.
63.3% > 60%, so her scoring rate has improved.

Question 6 — Holiday rebooking (6 marks)

Original Portugal holiday = £1200 (already paid). Alternative Greece holiday = £1800 before discounts, with a 20% rebooking incentive.

6a
£1440 after the 20% discount
Non-calculator method: 10% of £1800 = £180, so 20% = £360.
£1800 − £360 = £1440.
(Or: 80% of £1800 = £1440.)
6b
£1340 final price
£1440 − £100 (SUMMER code) = £1340.
6c
£70 extra each
Extra to pay overall = £1340 − £1200 = £140.
Split equally between two people: £140 ÷ 2 = £70 each.

Question 7 — Bike in two shops (3 marks)

Shop A: £198, 1/3 off. Shop B: £239, 40% off. Find the exact sale price in each shop and say which is cheaper.

7
Shop A = £132; Shop B = £143.40. Shop A is cheaper.
Shop A: 1/3 of £198 = £66 off. £198 − £66 = £132.
Shop B: 40% of £239 = 0.4 × 239 = £95.60 off. £239 − £95.60 = £143.40.
£132 < £143.40, so Shop A offers the lower price.

Question 8 — Percentage change (6 marks)

8a
£9000 after one year
Non-calculator method: 25% = 1/4. 1/4 of £12,000 = £3000.
£12,000 − £3000 = £9000.
(Or: 75% of £12,000 = £9000.)
8b
≈ 10.5% increase
Increase = 1.68 − 1.52 = 0.16 m.
% increase = (increase ÷ original) × 100
= 0.16 ÷ 1.52 × 100
= 10.526…% ≈ 10.5% (1 d.p.).
