Section A — Easy

01
(a) 2    (b) 3    (c) 3
(a) 8% of 25 = 25% of 8 = 2.
(b) 5% of 60 = 60% of 5 = 3.
(c) 2% of 150 = 150% of 2 = 3.
02
(a) 5/9    (b) 7/9
Let x = 0.5555…; 10x = 5.5555…; 9x = 5; x = 5/9. Same method gives 7/9 for (b).
03

(a) 4 = 2×2 → terminates (0.25). (b) denominator 3 → recurs (0.333…). (c) 10 = 2×5 → terminates (0.7).

09
(a) terminating    (b) recurring
(a) 40 = 2×2×2×5 — only 2s and 5s, so it terminates (0.175).
(b) 12 = 2×2×3 — the factor of 3 means it recurs (0.41666…).
10
(a) 4    (b) 4
A square-based pyramid has 4 planes of symmetry. An equilateral triangular prism also has 4 (three lengthways, plus one across the middle).
11
(a) rectangle 5 × 3    (b) rectangle 5 × 2    (c) rectangle 3 × 2
Plan (from above) uses width × depth = 5 × 3. Front elevation uses width × height = 5 × 2. Side elevation uses depth × height = 3 × 2.
12
(a) 23    (b) 21    (c) 19
Values: 14, 18, 21, 21, 25, 26, 30, 33 (8 values). Median = mean of the 4th and 5th = (21 + 25) ÷ 2 = 23. Mode = 21 (appears twice). Range = 33 − 14 = 19.

15
(a) the horizontal axis    (b) generally rising    (c) May → Jun
(a) Time (the months) always goes on the horizontal axis.
(b) Overall upward trend, with a small dip from Feb (14) to Mar (13).
(c) Rises: +2, −1, +5, +4, +8. The biggest is May 22 → Jun 30, a rise of 8.
16
(a) 34    (b) 22    (c) 2/5
Values: 23, 26, 28, 31, 34, 34, 37, 40, 42, 45 (10 values).
(a) Median = mean of the 5th and 6th = (34 + 34) ÷ 2 = 34.
(b) Range = 45 − 23 = 22.
(c) Below 34: 23, 26, 28, 31 = 4 values. 4/10 = 2/5.
