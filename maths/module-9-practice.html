Practice questions

Three difficulty levels. Have a proper go before checking the answers.

Notes Practice Answers Assignment

Section A — Easy

Warm-up

01

Use the trick x% of y = y% of x to calculate:

(a) 8% of 25 (b) 5% of 60 (c) 2% of 150
02

Write each recurring decimal as a fraction in its simplest form:

(a) 0.5555… (5 recurring) (b) 0.7777… (7 recurring)
03

Does each fraction give a terminating or a recurring decimal?

(a) 1/4 (b) 1/3 (c) 7/10
04

How many planes of symmetry does each shape have?

(a) a cube (b) a cuboid that is not a cube
05

Name the three standard 2D views used to represent a 3D solid.

06

A stem-and-leaf diagram of test marks:

1 | 2  7
2 | 0  4  4  9
3 | 5
Key: 1 | 2 means 12

(a) How many people took the test? (b) What is the smallest mark? (c) What is the largest mark?

Section B — Medium

Typical Year 8 standard

07

Use the trick to calculate:

(a) 4% of 75 (b) 12% of 25
08

Write 0.4545… (45 recurring) as a fraction in its simplest form.

09

Without dividing them out, say whether each fraction gives a terminating or recurring decimal, and explain how you can tell:

(a) 7/40 (b) 5/12
10

How many planes of symmetry does each shape have?

(a) a square-based pyramid (b) a triangular prism with an equilateral cross-section
11

A cuboid measures 5 cm wide, 3 cm deep and 2 cm tall. Give the shape and dimensions of its:

(a) plan (b) front elevation (c) side elevation
12

A stem-and-leaf diagram of daily temperatures (°C):

1 | 4  8
2 | 1  1  5  6
3 | 0  3
Key: 1 | 4 means 14

(a) Find the median (b) Find the mode (c) Find the range

Section C — Hard

Challenge / problem-solving

13

Using the trick throughout, work out 15% of 40 + 8% of 25.

14

Write 0.1666… (where only the 6 recurs) as a fraction in its simplest form. Hint: the 1 does not repeat, so line up the recurring 6 before subtracting.

15

A shop records its monthly sales (in £1000s):

Jan 12   Feb 14   Mar 13   Apr 18   May 22   Jun 30

(a) If drawn as a time series graph, which axis shows the months? (b) Describe the overall trend. (c) Between which two months was the biggest rise?
16

A stem-and-leaf diagram has 10 values. The leaves are:

2 | 3  6  8
3 | 1  4  4  7
4 | 0  2  5
Key: 2 | 3 means 23

(a) Find the median (mean of the two middle values) (b) Find the range (c) What fraction of the values are below 34? Give it in its simplest form.
