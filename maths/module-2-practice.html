Typical Year 8 standard

08

Section C — Hard

Challenge / problem-solving

13

Without a calculator: 0.6 × 1.4 + 2.16 ÷ 0.4

14

The line y = mx + c passes through (1, 5) and (4, 14). Find m and c.

15

A swimming pool fills at a constant rate. After 10 minutes it contains 60 litres. After 25 minutes it contains 150 litres.

(a) Find the rate (litres per minute) (b) How much was already in the pool when filling started? (c) Write an equation for V (litres) in terms of t (minutes)
16

Two phone tariffs: A charges £10 + £0.05 per minute. B charges £4 + £0.08 per minute. After how many minutes do they cost the same?
