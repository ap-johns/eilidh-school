Section A — Easy

Warm-up

01

A quadrilateral has angles 90°, 90°, 110°. What is the fourth angle?

02

(a) the corresponding angle (b) the alternate angle (c) the co-interior angle
03

Calculate the sum of interior angles of a pentagon (5 sides).

04

Each exterior angle of a regular polygon is 40°. How many sides does it have?

05

Write the bearing for:

(a) East (b) South-west (c) North
06

Section B — Medium

Typical Year 8 standard

07

The angles of a quadrilateral are x, 2x, x + 30 and 90. Find x.

08

Find the size of each interior angle of a regular octagon (8 sides).

09

Two parallel lines are crossed by a transversal. The two angles formed on the same side of the transversal between the parallel lines are 3x and 2x + 20. Find x.

10

Town B is on a bearing of 050° from town A, 12km away. Sketch the situation. Estimate the bearing of A from B.

11

A map has scale 1 : 25 000. Two villages are 4.4cm apart on the map. Real distance in km?

Section C — Hard

Challenge / problem-solving

12

The interior angles of a polygon are 130°, 140°, 150°, 160° and the rest are equal. The polygon has 8 sides. Find the size of each of the remaining angles.

13

The interior angle of a regular polygon is 144°. How many sides has it?

14

A ship sails on a bearing of 060° for 8km, then on a bearing of 150° for 6km. Sketch the path. What is the bearing of the start point from the ship's final position?

15

Construct a triangle with sides 6cm, 5cm and 4cm using ruler and compasses. Measure and state the largest angle. (Describe the construction steps.)
